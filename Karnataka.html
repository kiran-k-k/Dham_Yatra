Famous Temples of Karnataka

Karnataka

Virupaksha Temple

📍 Hampi 🕉️ Lord Shiva

Located in Hampi, this UNESCO World Heritage site is dedicated to Lord Shiva and showcases the magnificent Dravidian architectural style of the Vijayanagara Empire.

Plan Visit
Karnataka

Chamundeshwari Temple

📍 Mysore 🕉️ Goddess Chamundeshwari

Situated atop the sacred Chamundi Hills in Mysore, this temple is dedicated to Goddess Chamundeshwari and features a magnificent gopuram structure.

Plan Visit
Karnataka

Murudeshwar Temple

📍 Murudeshwar 🕉️ Lord Shiva

Famous for hosting the second tallest Shiva statue in the world, this coastal temple sits on Kanduka Hill, surrounded on three sides by the Arabian Sea.

Plan Visit
Karnataka

Hoysaleswara Temple

📍 Halebidu 🕉️ Lord Shiva

Located in Halebidu, this magnificent 12th-century temple is renowned for its highly detailed relief stone carvings and Hoysala architectural excellence.

Plan Visit
Karnataka

Udupi Krishna Temple

📍 Udupi 🕉️ Lord Krishna

A famous pilgrimage center dedicated to Lord Krishna, known for its unique tradition of viewing the deity through a silver-plated window (Kanakana Kindi).

Plan Visit
Karnataka

Gokarna Mahabaleshwar Temple

📍 Gokarna 🕉️ Lord Shiva

One of the most important Shiva temples in South India, located in the coastal town of Gokarna and holding the sacred Atma Linga of Lord Shiva.
