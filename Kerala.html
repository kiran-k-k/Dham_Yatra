Padmanabhaswamy Temple

📍 Trivandrum 🕉️ Lord Vishnu

Located in Thiruvananthapuram, this historic temple is dedicated to Lord Vishnu in the Anantha Shayana posture and is known globally for its spiritual wealth.

Sabarimala Temple

📍 Pathanamthitta 🕉️ Lord Ayyappa

Dedicated to Lord Ayyappa, this hilltop shrine in the Western Ghats is one of the largest annual pilgrimage destinations in the world.

Guruvayur Temple

📍 Guruvayur 🕉️ Lord Krishna

Known as 'Dwarka of the South', this temple is dedicated to Lord Krishna and is highly revered for its divine energy, healing traditions, and elephant sanctuary.

Kerala

Attukal Bhagavathy Temple

📍 Trivandrum 🕉️ Goddess Attukal Bhagavathy

Located in Thiruvananthapuram, this temple is famous for the Attukal Pongala festival, which holds a Guinness World Record for the largest gathering of women.

Kerala

Chottanikkara Temple

📍 Kochi 🕉️ Goddess Rajarajeswari

Dedicated to Goddess Rajarajeswari (Adiparasakthi), this temple near Kochi is famous for its healing powers and is visited by thousands of devotees daily.

Kerala

Vadakkunnathan Temple

📍 Thrissur 🕉️ Lord Shiva

Located in Thrissur, this ancient temple dedicated to Lord Shiva features traditional wooden Kerala architecture and is the epicenter of the famous Thrissur Pooram.
